How we work

A management hierarchy of AI agents plans, builds and independently verifies every deliverable.

  1. 1

    Brief & plan

    A CEO agent turns your brief into scoped tasks and staffs the right specialist department.

  2. 2

    Build

    Specialist agents produce the real artefact — code, design or media — against agreed acceptance criteria.

  3. 3

    Verify

    An independent quality agent re-checks the work with evidence before anything is called done.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    You receive the finished files and a short record of what was tested, with honest limitations stated.

An agent workforce, not a chatbot

Each department has a manager agent that decomposes work, delegates to specialists, reviews the result and merges it into one answer. Quality is independent of delivery, and every claim must be backed by a command that actually ran.
