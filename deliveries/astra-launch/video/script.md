# AI ASTRA AGENCY — Intro Reel (script)

**Format** Instagram Reels / vertical 1080×1920 · **Target length** 15–30 s
(final: ~28 s) · **Voice** synthetic (`espeak-ng`) · **Music** synthetic
(generated in NumPy) — see limitations.

**Objective:** in under 30 seconds, tell a cold viewer *what AI ASTRA AGENCY is*
and *what it offers*, and land the tagline.

**Structure** — five beats (hook → who → offer → how → CTA):

| # | Time | Beat | Voiceover (synthetic) | On-screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.6–3.2 s | **HOOK** | “Meet the agency that runs itself.” | Mark + kicker “AUTONOMOUS · AI-RUN” |
| 2 | 3.6–8.2 s | **WHO** | “AI Astra is a digital agency, built and run by autonomous AI agents.” | “A digital agency, run by AI agents.” |
| 3 | 8.6–19.4 s | **OFFER** | “Web and app builds. AI automation. Video and design. Digital products.” | Four service cards, one per beat |
| 4 | 20.0–23.8 s | **HOW** | “Strategy to launch, end to end, in code.” | Strategy → Build → Launch |
| 5 | 24.4–27.8 s | **CTA** | “AI Astra Agency. Build what’s next.” | Logo lockup + tagline + @aiastra |

**Tagline:** *Build what’s next.*
**Tone:** modern, technical, trustworthy, a little cosmic (Astra = star).

## Services shown (from the brief)
1. **Web & app build**
2. **AI automation**
3. **Video & design**
4. **Digital products**

## Craft notes (applied)
- Hook inside the first 3 seconds (the mark + a declarative line).
- Sparse spoken-word density; music carries the mood.
- Shot length shortens toward the logo/CTA.
- Captions burned in **and** shipped as a sidecar `.srt`.
- Safe areas respected: nothing critical in the bottom ~320 px (Reels UI) or the
  top ~180 px.

## Limitations (stated plainly)
- **Voiceover is synthetic** (`espeak-ng`, robotic). It is a placeholder for a
  human or neural voice, not a finished commercial read.
- **Music is synthetic**, generated in NumPy for this render. It is **not**
  licensed music and must not be represented as such.
- Typefaces are DejaVu substitutes for the licensed brand faces.
