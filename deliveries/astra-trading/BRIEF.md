# Astra Trading — Product Brief (v1)

**Name:** Astra Trading
**Company:** AI ASTRA AGENCY
**Tagline (EN):** *Gold, decoded.*
**Languages:** Arabic (ar) · English (en) · Turkish (tr) — full RTL for Arabic
**Stage:** Planning → Brand identity → Build

## What it is
An AI-powered gold (XAU/USD) analysis SaaS. It maps support/resistance and
liquidity zones automatically, computes volatility and session context, and
publishes **AI-proposed setups** (entry / stop / targets / R:R) with a
transparent, auditable track record. Analysis only — never investment advice,
never trade execution.

## Languages (required)
- **Arabic** — primary, full RTL, Levantine-friendly plain wording
- **English** — global
- **Turkish** — large gold-trading market
Every screen, email and notification must support all three.

## Deliverables this phase (brand identity first)
1. **Brand identity** (Creative dept): logo + wordmark (3 languages), palette,
   type, iconography, chart/UI colour language (bull/bear/neutral), motion.
   Must read well on dark trading UI and in small sizes.
2. **Owner approval on Telegram** before any build starts.

## Hard constraints
- Financial-risk sensitivity: no "signals" framing, no profit promises; show
  disclaimers. Brand must look **credible and institutional**, not "get-rich".
- Must not clash with AI ASTRA AGENCY corporate brand — it's a **product** brand
  under it (sub-brand relationship).
- RTL must be designed, not mirrored as an afterthought.
- Turkish has special characters (ğ, ş, ı, İ, ö, ü, ç) — font must support them.
- Arabic script needs a font with proper Arabic glyph coverage.
