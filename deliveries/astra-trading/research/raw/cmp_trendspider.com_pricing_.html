Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers! If you need more help, feel free to reach out.

TrendSpider offers four pre-built plans: Standard, Premium, Enhanced, and Advanced. Each plan includes full access to all of TrendSpider’s tools and features, as well as the same market data access. The difference lies in usage limits, which are displayed in the plan boxes at the top of the pricing page. As a general rule, the more active and systematic your trading is, the higher your plan should be. If you're unsure, start with Standard and upgrade later if needed.

Plan pricing varies based on the tier and whether you pay monthly or annually. Annual plans offer a discount over monthly billing. To see the latest pricing, visit the plan descriptions at the top of this page. Certain features are offered for additional fees. Please see the “Add-ons and upgrades” section above for more information.

Yes! We offer a 14-day paid trial for a small fee, which depends on which plan you wish to try. Paid trials are a win-win. They help us limit the number of trials so we can devote more time and resources to helping serious traders get started, while giving you a chance to fully test the platform without hurry. If you don’t wish to continue, you can cancel before the trial ends to avoid further charges.

TrendSpider combines professional-grade market data, automation, and AI-driven analysis tools into a single platform. Instead of paying for multiple separate services, you get everything in one system. Our pricing reflects the cost of premium data feeds, intelligent scanning, automated alerts, and other advanced features that help traders make informed decisions.

Non-professional traders (such as individual retail traders who trade their own accounts) will not incure any additional fees for real-time U.S. equities, ETFs, forex, and crypto data. Real-time futures data is optional, and can be purchased by non-professional users for $7.50/month or $90/year. Professional traders are required to pay a NASDAQ exchange fee of $29 per month in order to access US Equity real-time or historical data.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, Discover and American Express credit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. More payment options, including cryptocurrency, may be added in the future.

Yes, subscriptions auto-renew by default to prevent service interruption. You can disable auto-renewal anytime in your Account & Billing settings.

You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription anytime. Log into your TrendSpider account, navigate to Account & Billing, and modify your plan as needed. Plan changes take effect immediately (upgrades) or at the end of your billing cycle (downgrades/cancellations).

Trial fees are non-refundable. However, if your trial renews or you purchase a full plan and change your mind within 72 hours, you can request a refund (minus the trial fee). Refund requests must be submitted within 72 hours of the initial charge. Because by default when you cancel, you disable future automated renewals but your account stays active for your pre-paid time, if you wish to request a refund please contact us after cancelling your plan.

If a payment fails, we’ll notify you to update your payment method. Once your payment goes past due, you are allowed a 72 hour grace period. If the payment remains unresolved, your subscription may be suspended until payment is received. Our billing provider will automatically retry the payment multiple times over a four-week period. If we are unable to process your payment after this period of time, your account will be canceled and shut down.

TrendSpider is a cloud-based platform accessible via any web browser. No software installation is required.

Yes, TrendSpider is available on Android and iOS through our mobile apps. Some complex features like creating alerts or new scanners are best used on desktop, but you can still view scanner results, alerts, and charts on mobile.

We use industry-standard encryption to protect user data, and all payment details are securely processed by Stripe and PayPal, two industry leading payment processing companies. Your account security is a top priority.

TrendSpider continuously improves its platform with regular updates, often weekly. New features, enhancements, and fixes are automatically applied—you don't need to install anything manually.

We pride ourselves on providing white-glove service and support. If you need help learning to use TrendSpider, we have countless resources ranging from self-help videos and documentation, to dedicated 1-on-1 help sessions and training sessions.
