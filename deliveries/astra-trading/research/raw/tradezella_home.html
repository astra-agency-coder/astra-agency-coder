Frequently Asked Questions

What is a trading journal?

A trading journal is a record of every trade you take: entry, exit, position size, setup, and the reasoning behind it. Traders use it to find which setups make money and which habits cost them. A trading journal like TradeZella imports your trades automatically, and Zella AI tags every trade and reviews every session, so your journal stays complete without manual logging.

How do I use TradeZella’s trade journal?

Import your trades, set up your Strategies, and let Zella AI handle the rest. TradeZella imports trades from 500+ brokers through auto-sync or CSV upload. You define your Strategies and tagging rules once. From there, Zella AI builds your pre-market plan with Start My Day, the Auto-Tagger tags every trade by your rules, and Session Review journals your day by comparing your plan to your results. Your weekly review takes about 30 minutes in the analytics dashboard.

Who is TradeZella for?

TradeZella is built for every trader. Day trading, swing trading, prop firm challenges, or your first month in the markets, it works with stocks, futures, forex, options, and crypto. Sign up here to get started.

What brokers do you support?

TradeZella supports 500+ brokers and trading platforms across stocks, futures, forex, options, and crypto. That includes Interactive Brokers, Schwab, Webull, Robinhood, MT4, MT5, Coinbase, and Bybit. Some brokers sync automatically, and others import through a quick CSV upload. See the full broker list.

Can I use TradeZella as a forex trading journal?

Yes. TradeZella works as a full forex trading journal, with MT4 and MT5 imports through API or CSV. You can track performance by currency pair and by session (London, New York, Asian, and overlap), and factor in spread and swap costs that spreadsheets usually miss. Forex prop firm traders can run evaluation accounts through Prop Firm Sync.

Should I keep my trade journal in Excel or use a trading journal app?

Excel works if you take a few trades a week and like building your own formulas. Once you take 5 or more trades a day or trade across multiple accounts, manual entry takes hours each week and small errors start skewing your stats. A trading journal app imports trades automatically and calculates win rate, profit factor, and R-multiples for you. With TradeZella, Zella AI also tags and reviews every trade, so the journaling gets done even on days you would skip it. See the full trading journal vs spreadsheet comparison.

Is there a free trading journal template I can start with?

Yes. You can download a free Trading Journal Template, along with a Trading Checklist and Trading Plan Template, from our free trading tools page. A template is a solid way to build the journaling habit. When manual entry starts slowing you down, TradeZella imports your trades automatically and Zella AI handles the tagging and reviews.
