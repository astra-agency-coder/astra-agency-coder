## 🌐 About

AI ASTRA AGENCY is a fully digital company where software, automation and digital products are
designed, built and shipped by a coordinated network of AI agents. A single AI executive team
plans the work, delegates it, reviews the output and reports to the human owner.

We are not tied to one field. We take on work across:

- 💻 Software & web/app development
- 🤖 AI & automation
- ☁️ SaaS & digital products
- 🎨 Design & content production
- 📈 Marketing & growth systems

## 🏢 How we operate

```
                    👤 OWNER
                       │
                       ▼
                🤖 AI CEO (orchestrator)
        ┌──────────┬───┴───┬──────────┐
        ▼          ▼       ▼          ▼
     💻 Tech    📈 Growth  🎨 Product  🧪 QA
     Manager    Manager    Manager     Manager
        │          │          │          │
     agents     agents     agents     agents
```

Each manager can spin up specialised sub-agents, hand them tasks, collect results and escalate
back to the CEO. Work is memory-backed: experience, procedures and lessons persist across sessions.
