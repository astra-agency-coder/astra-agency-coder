verifies, and improves, using a workforce of specialised AI agents — and produces
real, verifiable value for real customers.

products, markets and revenue models based on feasibility, capacity and expected
return. Candidate domains: software & web/app development, AI & automation, SaaS,
digital products & licensing, marketing & content, design & video, data & research.
