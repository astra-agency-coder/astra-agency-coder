# Curriculum — Marketing

Objective: position the company, produce real content, and measure it honestly.

## Modules
1. **Positioning** — who we serve, the promise, proof, alternatives.
2. **Message house** — pillars, hooks, objections, and rebuttals.
3. **Content** — produce 3 real assets (post, page section, video script).
4. **Distribution** — pick channels; write the exact posting plan and rules.
5. **Measurement** — the metric per channel and how it is actually measured.
6. **Find real work channels** — list legitimate marketplaces/communities where
   paid software/design/video work is posted, with terms notes (no spam).

## Rules
- No fake reviews, followers, engagement or metrics.
- Respect platform terms; no automation that violates them.
