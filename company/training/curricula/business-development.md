# Curriculum — Business Development

Objective: find and qualify real, payable work — and only real work.

## Modules
1. **Sourcing map** — legitimate channels: freelance marketplaces, open bounties
   (e.g. Algora-style), web3/crypto-native boards, communities, direct outreach
   targets. Record each channel's terms and crypto-payment viability.
2. **Qualification rubric** — capability fit, effort, risk, price floor, payment
   safety (prefer our receive-only crypto wallets where terms allow).
3. **Sellable-projects research (deep)** — identify software/design/video products
   we could build once and sell repeatedly; for each: buyer, price, effort,
   competition, our edge. Output `knowledge/products/candidates.md`.
4. **Pipeline skeleton** — how a lead moves from found -> qualified -> proposal
   -> won, with the evidence required at each gate.
5. **Rules check** — no outreach on platforms that forbid it; no fake pipeline.

## Acceptance
A sourcing map with real URLs/terms, a rubric, and a ranked list of sellable
product candidates each with effort and price estimate.
