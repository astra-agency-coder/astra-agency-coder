# Curriculum — Research

Objective: deliver intelligence that other departments can act on.

## Modules
1. **Method** — how to source, verify and cite (primary > secondary).
2. **Market study** — one real market: size signals, demand evidence, key players.
3. **Technical study** — feasibility of one tech bet (e.g. AI video pipeline).
4. **Competitive scan** — who else does this, their pricing, their gaps.
5. **Brief** — one-page decision brief with sources and clear uncertainty.

## Rules
- Every claim carries a source; fact vs inference is labelled. Never fabricate.
