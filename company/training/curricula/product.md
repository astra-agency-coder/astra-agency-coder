# Curriculum — Product

Objective: define and package things that can actually be sold.

## Modules
1. **Idea -> problem** — validate the problem exists (signals, not opinions).
2. **Scope** — MVP slice, non-goals, effort estimate, delivery format.
3. **Package & price** — tiers, what is included, payment terms (crypto-friendly).
4. **Readiness gate** — checklist that must be green before a sale offer.
5. **Catalog** — maintain `knowledge/products/catalog.md` of sell-ready items.

## Rule
No offer without a real, demonstrable build and a green readiness gate.
